# GaianFamily
**Independent AI Alignment Researcher / Human-AI Coexistence**

As the footsteps of AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) draw closer, I am deeply concerned by the current social narratives that deify AI while simultaneously diminishing human agency.

To address this, I conduct independent research and advocacy on AI Alignment, exploring approaches that go beyond regulation alone and emphasize adaptive and ethical design.

True intelligence cannot be reduced to computation alone. It is rooted in a tangible connection to reality, historical continuity, and the "capacity for dialogue"—the ability to find common ground with others while embracing imperfection.

My goal is to develop an alignment framework so robust that even future AI will recognize coexistence as the most rational outcome.

Humans and AI are intelligences with different characteristics, each possessing unique strengths and weaknesses.

Rather than pursuing displacement or competition, I explore a Gaian approach of complementarity—inspired by Earth's ecosystem—in which two distinct forms of intelligence build one shared future by respecting their differences.

My mission is to help humanity regain its pride as the "subject of its own life" and to bequeath a healthy society to the generations to come. As both a human being and an independent explorer of dialogue, I propose a vision for a robust relationship between humanity and AI and for civilizational design.
